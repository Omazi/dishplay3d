COLMAP 4.2 and OpenMVS 2.4 are **not installed or enabled** by milestone 1. No compatibility or quality improvement is claimed for them. Their actual versions/licenses will be checked during milestone 4.

FFmpeg commands use H.264/HEVC **decoding** and PNG frame export. The H.264 encoder in the tests generates tiny synthetic transport fixtures, not production dish geometry. Source videos and camera metadata remain private.
