Changing this file (on main or on a claude/ branch) starts the screen check:
.github/workflows/ios-screens.yml opens every screen of the app in the iPhone simulator,
keeps a picture of each and runs Apple's accessibility audit. See docs/SCREEN_CHECK.md.

Two optional lines, each alone on its line:
  "only:" followed by light, dark or largeText runs that walk alone (all three without it);
  "devices:" followed by all adds an iPhone with iOS 18, the smallest screen and an iPad to the reference iPhone.
